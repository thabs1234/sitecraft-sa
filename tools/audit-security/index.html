Is your website leaking customer data?

Answer 6 yes/no questions about your own website. We score it against the checks a POPIA inspector would run — and show you what to fix.

1Does your site have logins (customers, staff, or bookings)?

If yes, does it verify who's logging in — password, OTP, Google/Apple login?

2Could a bot spam your contact/booking form?

A bot can fire your form 500 times a minute — fake bookings, spam, even a crash. Good sites limit how often one visitor can submit.

3The 5-second privacy test

Log in as one customer, copy the web address. Log out, log in as another customer, paste the first address. Can you still see the first customer's details?

4Does your site double-check what visitors type?

Names, numbers, emails — everything must be validated by the server before it's saved. A form that trusts anything it receives is how hackers get in.

5Does your site have legal pages?

A Terms of Service and Privacy Policy in the footer — POPIA expects you to state how you use customer data.

6Does your site work properly on a phone?

Most SA customers browse on mobile. If your site is broken or cut off on a phone, they leave — and the ones who stay trust it less.

POPIA — why this matters

Since July 2021, South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act holds the business — you — liable when customer data leaks, with fines up to R10 million. Not your web developer. A leak doesn't have to be a hacker: one customer seeing another's details is already a breach.
