# Auto-DM Sequence — "Comment SITE"

The DM is where the freebie converts. Keep it human, short, SA voice.
Automate on Instagram with ManyChat (free tier, keyword trigger "site").
On TikTok, send these manually (they're short — 30 seconds of typing).

---

## DM 1 — keyword response (instant)

> Thanks for the comment! 🔥
Here's your free 10-point website checklist (the exact one I use
> to build sites): https://thabs1234.github.io/sitecraft-sa/freebie.html
>
> Quick question — what does your business do? If it's one of the niches I
> build for, I'll send you a FREE sample homepage this week. No payment, no
> obligation — you keep it either way.
>
> (POPIA note: I'll only message you about this. Reply STOP anytime and I'll
> never message again.)

## DM 2 — freebie follow-up (24h later, if no reply)

> Hey! Did the checklist land okay? 👋
> Tip #3 is the one most business owners miss — it's the difference between a
> website that gets seen and one that gets lost.

## DM 3 — the sample offer (48h, if still silent)

> No stress if now's not the time. Just so you know: I build sample homepages
> for free every week — you see exactly what you'd get before paying anything.
> Say "SAMPLE" and I'll send the details. 🤝

## DM 4 — soft close (72h)

> Last one from me — I've got two build slots left this week. If you'd like the
> sample, say SAMPLE. If not, keep the checklist and good luck with the
> business! 💪

---

## Rules

- **24h response max** on every keyword comment. Speed = trust + algorithm.
- Every DM carries the freebie + the next step. Never a dead end.
- The sample is the closer (matches the OSM lead engine: build BEFORE pitch).
- STOP = stop. Log the opt-out. Never message again (POPIA).
