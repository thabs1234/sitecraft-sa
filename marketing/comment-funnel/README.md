# SiteCraft SA — Comment Funnel (TikTok / Instagram)

The Doctor AI play, adapted for SA local businesses. Every video ends with a
one-word keyword CTA. The keyword triggers an auto-DM with a freebie. The
freebie leads to the pitch. The pitch closes on WhatsApp (R1,850 once + R450/mo Care — matches the live site pricing).

```
VIDEO (hook) ──► COMMENT "SITE" ──► AUTO-DM (freebie) ──► PITCH (WhatsApp) ──► CLIENT
```

## The one keyword

Use **SITE** everywhere. Short, easy to type, unique to you. Do NOT switch
keywords between videos — the whole funnel depends on one muscle-memory word.
(Doctor AI uses "CLAUDE" in every single video, 70+ parts deep.)

## What you need (all free or cheap, in order)

1. **Videos**: AI avatar or slideshow — see `marketing/cam-clone/` for the
   full machine. Minimum to start: phone + CapCut, 2-3 min per video.
2. **DM automation**:
   - Instagram: **ManyChat** free tier — keyword comment → auto-DM.
   - TikTok: no free DM automation; the account owner replies manually from the
     app. You're already opening TikTok daily to publish drafts (Postiz UPLOAD
     method) — reply to comments in the same 5 minutes.
3. **Freebie**: the checklist in `freebie-checklist.md` (host it on GitHub
   Pages: `thabs1234.github.io/sitecraft-sa/freebie.html` once you add it, or
   send as a PDF from `pdf/`).
4. **Tracking**: count comments with the keyword per video + DMs replied.
   That's your conversion number (views → comments → DMs → pitches).

## Cadence

- Start: **1 video/day** for 2 weeks. Same hook formula, rotate pillars.
- Scale: 2-3/day across TikTok + IG once you see which pillar gets comments.
- Series rule (Doctor AI's #1 retention trick): once a hook works, make it a
  series — "Why SA small businesses don't have websites — part 4". Older parts
  keep pulling comments for months.

## Funnel hygiene (SA legal + platform rules)

- **POPIA**: anyone who engages gets a privacy note in the DM sequence and can
  opt out anytime ("SEND STOP and I'll never message you again").
- WhatsApp Business policy: only message people who engaged with you (commented
  or replied). Never cold-DM commenters on mass lists.
- Comment-section etiquette: reply to every keyword comment within 24h —
  TikTok/IG rank videos higher when the author engages.
- No fake stats. Say "most people check Google first" — never invent a
  percentage.

## Metrics that matter (check weekly)

| Metric | Target |
|---|---|
| Comment rate (comments / views) | > 1% (Doctor AI runs 1-2%) |
| Keyword comments per video | > 10 |
| DM → freebie sent | 100% within 24h |
| Freebie → "yes to sample" | > 30% |
| Sample → closed client | > 25% |
