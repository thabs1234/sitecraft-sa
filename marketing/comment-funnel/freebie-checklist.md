# The 10-Point Website Checklist (SA Edition)

The freebie. Host this as `freebie.html` on the SiteCraft site (GitHub Pages)
or export to PDF into `pdf/`. Keep it genuinely useful — it's the proof of
your expertise that gets the DM conversation started.

---

**1. Loads fast on cheap data.** Most SA visitors are on mobile + prepaid
data. If your site takes more than 3 seconds to load, people leave before it
finishes. One-page sites built with AI load in under a second.

**2. A WhatsApp button above the fold.** South Africans don't fill in contact
forms — they WhatsApp. The click-to-chat button (`wa.me/27...`) must be visible
without scrolling. This single button is responsible for most enquiries on
SA small-business sites.

**3. Google Maps embedded.** People search "plumber near me" — when they land
on your site they want to confirm you're close. Embed the map + a "Get
Directions" link. It also helps your Google ranking.

**4. Services with PRICES.** "From R450" beats "contact us for a quote" every
time. Price-anchored services filter tyre-kickers and attract serious buyers.
No price = no trust in SA small business.

**5. A real photo of YOU or your work.** Stock photos scream "template".
One genuine photo of the owner or the shop builds more trust than any
marketing copy. AI can polish it, but start from something real.

**6. Your phone number clickable.** On mobile, a number must open the dialer
with one tap. If a customer has to copy-paste a number, you've lost half of
them. Same rule for email.

**7. A clear "What happens next".** Tell them exactly what happens after they
message you: "Reply in 5 minutes during business hours" or "We confirm within
the hour". Dead ends kill enquiries.

**8. Testimonials with names.** "Great job — Thandi, Soweto" beats a generic
five-star row. Ask every happy client for one sentence + a first name. It's
the cheapest marketing you'll ever buy.

**9. Your own domain (.co.za).** A `.co.za` domain (~R120/year) says "real
business". Free domains and weebly-style subdomains say "maybe a scam".
The R120 is the best ad spend in your budget.

**10. A review link after delivery.** After you finish a job, WhatsApp the
client a Google review link. Reviews are the #1 local ranking factor and you
only get them by asking, every time, on the spot.
