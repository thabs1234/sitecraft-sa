## Caption CTA rules
- First word of the caption = the promise. Last word = "SITE".
- Never write "link in bio" without the comment CTA — comments > link clicks
  (comments feed the algorithm AND give you DM opt-in).
- Reply to every keyword comment yourself for the first 30 days (learns the
  funnel, boosts ranking). Then automate.
