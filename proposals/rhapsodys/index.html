1 · The situation

Rhapsody's Sunnypark is listed and findable at Sunnypark Mall, Esselen Street, Sunnyside, Pretoria, with a working phone number — but there is no website attached to that listing. Everything below follows from that single gap.

Here is specifically what I found when I looked you up the way a customer would:

  • You have over 3 800 Facebook reviews with an 80% recommend rate and a 3.8 Tripadvisor rating — that is a serious reputation asset, and it currently lives entirely on platforms you don't control.
  • There is no website of your own. Every search for your name lands on Facebook, Tripadvisor or a third-party aggregator that monetises your traffic and sells ads to your competitors alongside it.
  • Your menu is not online in a form you control. For a restaurant, menu is the single most-searched item — diners decide before they leave the house.
  • TikTok users are actively calling you the top breakfast spot in Pretoria. That organic attention has nowhere to land and no booking path.
  • No online booking means every reservation depends on someone answering the phone during service.

This isn't a hypothetical. Someone searching for a fine-dining restaurant and cocktail lounge in your area today sees your name, finds nothing to click, and taps the competitor listed below you who does have a link. That happens quietly, every day, and nothing about it shows up in your books.
