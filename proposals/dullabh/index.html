1 · The situation

Prof. H.D. Dullabh Dental Surgery is listed and findable at 36 Lilian Ngoyi Street, Fordsburg, Johannesburg, with a working phone number — but there is no website attached to that listing. Everything below follows from that single gap.

Here is specifically what I found when I looked you up the way a customer would:

  • Your practice appears on Google Maps, Medpages and Cybo with the correct address and phone number — but every one of those listings has an empty website field.
  • You are listed as a registered specialist prosthodontist, one of a small number in Johannesburg. That is a genuinely rare credential and nothing online communicates it to a patient searching for help.
  • Patients searching "crowns Johannesburg" or "dentures Fordsburg" are shown general dentists with websites ahead of a specialist without one.
  • Referring general dentists have nothing to send a patient to. A referral link is the single easiest way to convert a referral into a booking.
  • There is no page explaining what a prosthodontist actually does — so patients who need you don't know they need you.

This isn't a hypothetical. Someone searching for a specialist prosthodontic practice in your area today sees your name, finds nothing to click, and taps the competitor listed below you who does have a link. That happens quietly, every day, and nothing about it shows up in your books.
