# gbp-setup — Google Business Profile wizard

## Why it matters
A website with no Google Business Profile is half-built. GBP is what makes a
business show up in the map pack when locals search "salon near me". It's free
and it's the single highest-leverage local-SEO action.

## Inputs
- `name`, `type`, `area`, `phone`, `address` (or suburb if no street yet), `hours`

## Steps to hand the client (or do for them)
1. Go to google.com/business and sign in with their Google account.
2. Enter the **exact** business name (match the site + directories — NAP consistency).
3. Category: choose the closest primary category (e.g. "Hair salon", "Restaurant", "Auto repair shop").
4. Area: if no storefront visits, set "service area" to {area}; else enter the address (hide street if home-based).
5. Phone: {phone} (tap-to-call). Add a WhatsApp number if available.
6. Hours: real open hours (keep updated — wrong hours = lost trust).
7. Verify: postcard / phone / email — tell them to expect it and not to skip it.
8. Add 5–10 real photos (storefront, team, work). Photos = more calls.
9. Write a 1-line intro using the sitecopy hero sub.
10. Publish, then post 1 update/week (specials, hours, thanks) — keeps the listing fresh.

## Handoff bundle (what SiteCraft delivers in setup)
- The profile info pre-filled in a sheet (`gbp-<slug>.txt`).
- The verified listing linked from the live site's "Find us" button.
- First 2 GBP posts drafted.

## Rule
Never claim "I'll verify for you" if you can't access their Google account — hand
them the pre-filled sheet + a 2-minute Loom-style step list instead. Verification
must be done on their account.
