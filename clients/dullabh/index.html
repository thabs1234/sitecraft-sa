Common questions

Do I need a referral to be seen?

No. Many patients come through a referral from their general dentist, but you are welcome to book a specialist consultation directly.

What is a prosthodontist?

A dentist with additional specialist training in restoring and replacing teeth — crowns, bridges, dentures and implant restorations.

Do you claim from medical aid?

Yes. We submit directly to most major schemes and provide full itemised quotes with tariff codes upfront.

How long does a crown take?

Usually two visits — preparation and impressions on the first, permanent fitting on the second, generally within two weeks.
