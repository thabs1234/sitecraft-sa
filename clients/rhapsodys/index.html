Common questions

Do you take bookings?

Yes — phone ahead, especially for Friday and Saturday evenings and weekend breakfast, which fill quickly.

Is there parking?

Yes. Secure parking at Sunnypark Mall, with direct access to the restaurant.

Can you host a private function?

Absolutely. We handle birthdays, corporate evenings and celebrations with set menus — call to discuss numbers.

What time does the kitchen close?

The kitchen runs until 22:00 on weeknights and later on Fridays and Saturdays; the lounge stays open past the kitchen.
