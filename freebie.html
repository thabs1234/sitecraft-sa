Run through all ten

Written for the South African market: mobile-first, prepaid-data friendly, WhatsApp-native. No jargon.

  1. Loads fast on cheap data

    Most visitors are on mobile with prepaid data. Over three seconds and they're gone. A one-page AI-built site loads in under a second.

  2. A WhatsApp button above the fold

    South Africans don't fill in contact forms — they WhatsApp. The click-to-chat button must be visible without scrolling. This one button drives most enquiries on SA small-business sites.

  3. Google Maps embedded

    People search "plumber near me" and want to confirm you're close. Map + "get directions" link, on the page. It also helps your Google ranking.

  4. Services with prices

    "From R450" beats "contact us for a quote" every time. Prices filter tyre-kickers and attract serious buyers. No price = no trust.

  5. A real photo of you or your work

    Stock photos scream template. One genuine photo of the owner or the shop builds more trust than any copy. AI can polish it — start from something real.

  6. A clickable phone number

    On mobile, the number must open the dialer in one tap. If a customer has to copy-paste, you've lost half of them. Same rule for email.

  7. Say what happens next

    Tell them exactly what follows: "We reply within the hour" or "Message us and we confirm today". Dead ends kill enquiries.

  8. Testimonials with names

    "Great job — Thandi, Soweto" beats a generic five-star row. Ask every happy client for one sentence and a first name. Cheapest marketing you'll ever buy.

  9. Your own .co.za domain

    A .co.za (~R120/year) says "real business". Free subdomains say "maybe a scam". It's the best R120 in your budget.

  10. A review link after delivery

    After every job, WhatsApp the client a Google review link. Reviews are the #1 local ranking factor — and you only get them by asking, every time, on the spot.
